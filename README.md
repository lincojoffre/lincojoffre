## Flagship product — LINSender

**LINSender** is a business communication and workflow automation platform being developed by Joffre Negócios Digitais.

Its purpose is to centralize customer communication, lead management, intelligent workflows and operational visibility in a single modular environment.

The product is designed for businesses that need to automate communication without losing control over customer data, process status, human intervention and operational decisions.

### Product vision

LINSender is being designed as more than a message-sending tool.

Its long-term vision is to become a business operating platform capable of connecting:

* Customer communication
* Lead and customer data
* Commercial workflows
* Artificial intelligence
* Follow-up processes
* Operational analytics
* Human service teams
* External business systems

## How I approach software

My experience in administrative, digital and operational environments influences how I design systems.

Before developing a solution, I try to answer:

```text
Who will use the system?
Which problem is actually being solved?
What process already exists?
Which decisions can be automated?
Which decisions require human approval?
What happens when an integration fails?
Which data must remain visible?
How will the system be maintained?
How will its results be measured?
```

This perspective helps connect technical implementation with operational value.

---

## Engineering principles

```text
Understand before automating.
Design for people as well as systems.
Prefer clarity over unnecessary complexity.
Make important decisions observable.
Treat security as a requirement.
Keep credentials outside the source code.
Document the reasoning, not only the result.
Build incrementally and validate continuously.
Use AI as a component, not as the entire architecture.
```
